Scalpel Stream

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Scalpel is free, open-source software (source code, AGPL-3.0) made by volunteers. It is not a business and sells nothing. This policy covers Scalpel Stream: the Stream feature of the Scalpel desktop app, the Scalpel Stream Twitch extension, and the gear pages at live.scalpel.fourth.party.

What is stored

Only for streamers who turn Scalpel Stream on:

  • A random profile ID, and a hash of the key Scalpel uses to publish to it.
  • The Twitch channel name you enter, and your Twitch channel ID if you link the Twitch extension.
  • Snapshots of your Path of Exile 2 character as Scalpel publishes them: character name (unless you hide it), class, level, league, equipped items, jewels, skills, keystones and a Path of Building code, with the time each was published. This comes from public build data on poe.ninja and is shown publicly by design.

Viewers

The extension and the gear pages have no accounts, set no cookies, and use no analytics or advertising. Twitch gives the extension a token that proves a request comes from Twitch; viewer identities are not stored.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare hosts the service and keeps standard request logs, including IP addresses, under its own policy.
  • Twitch runs the extension platform under its own privacy notice.
  • Item art loads from web.poecdn.com (Grinding Gear Games), and class and keystone art from assets.poe.ninja (poe.ninja), so those services receive your browser's requests for it.
  • Character data comes from poe.ninja's public build data.

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Keeping and deleting data

Stored data stays until you delete it. "Remove my Scalpel Stream data" in Scalpel (Settings, Stream) deletes your profile, every snapshot and any Twitch link. "Unlink this channel" in the extension's configuration removes just the Twitch link. Cloudflare's logs expire on Cloudflare's schedule.

Children

Scalpel Stream is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Changes are posted on this page with a new date.
